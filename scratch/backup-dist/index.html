Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How can an authorized representative claim my document?
Authorized representatives must bring: (1) Signed Authorization Letter from the student/alumnus, (2) Photocopy of the student's valid ID with 3 specimen signatures, and (3) The representative's original valid government ID.
Can I request official Transcript of Records if I have a pending clearance?
No, all university clearances must be settled before you can request official documents like the Transcript of Records.
How do I track my nationwide courier delivery parcel?
Once your document is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email and on your portal dashboard to monitor its delivery status.
What are the acceptable online payment methods?
We currently accept payments through standard online banking channels, credit/debit cards, and major e-wallets like GCash and Maya.
